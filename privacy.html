Privacy policy

family-cal-labeler is used only by its owner's household. It reads events on one shared family calendar and edits only their titles to add the name of the person who created them.

No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Event data is processed on the owner's own server and is not stored beyond a local log of changes.

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
